## How ZgoVPS Pricing Is Structured

This is the part that confuses newcomers, so let's clear it up first. ZgoVPS doesn't sell one generic "VPS." Pricing varies by three big levers:

- **Data center location** — Los Angeles, Osaka, Tokyo, Hong Kong, Falkenstein. Each has different cost bases.
- **Network route** — China-optimized (CN2 GIA / AS9929 / CMIN2) costs more than "Global" international routing. Dual-ISP IP plans sit at the premium end.
- **Billing cycle** — yearly is dramatically cheaper per month than quarterly, and special-offer yearly plans are where the real bargains live.

Most plans bill in **USD**, accepted via PayPal, Alipay, and credit card. Special-offer (特价) plans are non-refundable and can't stack with promo codes, so read the fine print before clicking buy.

## A Few Honest Caveats

ZgoCloud uses an automated MaxMind fraud check on orders. Your IP, phone number, and selected country need to be internally consistent (they don't need to be real, just consistent) or the order gets flagged and you can't complete checkout. It's a common annoyance with WHMCS-based hosts — worth knowing before you start filling out the form.

Special-offer plans are explicitly non-refundable, and the Frankfurt international-routed plans aren't China-optimized — ZgoCloud states upfront that you can't refund on those grounds. So if you're buying blind, stick to a regular plan first, test routes via their looking glass, and only then commit to a yearly special.
